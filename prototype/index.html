A matched ride should stay on track. Explore how reason-specific driver interventions, priority rematching, and a dedicated spiral metric could help a marketplace recover when it does not.

RIDER EXPERIENCE / 01

Recover the moment
trust is at risk.

When a driver cancels after match, the rider sees what happened and moves into priority rematching. The experience shows a range for the next match instead of a precise promise the product cannot guarantee.
